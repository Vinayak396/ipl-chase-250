Stadium Pitch Rules & Constraints

1. The Scenario

The opposition has posted an immaculate total of 250/0 in 20.0 overs. You must draft an all-time IPL XI to chase down 251 runs in 120 deliveries (RRR: 12.55).

2. 11 Blank Spots on the Pitch

You have 11 blank spots representing batting positions #1 through #11. Whenever you select a player from the random squad on the left, you have complete freedom to place them into any spot (1-11) you choose!

3. Team Quota: Max 2 Players per Franchise

You can pick players from different teams and eras, but you can draft at most 2 players from any single team (e.g. max 2 from RCB, max 2 from MI, max 2 from CSK, etc.).

4. No Duplicate Players

You cannot select the same player twice, even across different seasons (e.g. if you draft Virat Kohli from RCB 2016, all other Virat Kohli cards are locked).

5. Foreign Player Limit: Max 4 Overseas

Strictly adhering to official IPL rules, your playing 11 can contain a maximum of 4 foreign (✈️ Overseas) players. Once 4 are placed, foreign players are automatically locked.

6. The 3 Core 500-0 Threshold Gauges

  • Batting Survival (Top 7): Target 600+ across slots #1 to #7.
  • Power Acceleration (Top 7): Target 645+ across your top 7 hitters to sustain 12.55 RPO!
  • Bowling & Morale (Top 4): Target 350+ across your top 4 bowlers.
